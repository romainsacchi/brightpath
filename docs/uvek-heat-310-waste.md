# Exact 3.10 waste and recycled-polymer profiles

The HDPE market, hazardous-waste market and Swiss hazardous-incineration service
are independently qualified for ecoinvent 3.10 cut-off with native UVEK suppliers.
Other releases and system models cannot borrow these permissions.

The new HDPE helper retains both original virgin producer entries separately,
without normalisation, plus all three original collection/delivery freight entries.
Four positive recycled-to-generic supplies, each 2.93285310007683e-14 kg/kg, are
explicitly omitted under the standing approval of similar independently inspected
cases. Each actual 3.10 supplier has a nonempty upstream recycled-material input.
The omission therefore remains a physical loss with unquantified score effect;
it is not represented by empty recycling, zero burden or extra virgin material.
Fresh audits record the literal portable source exchanges, input indices, actual
3.10 profile and inventory hash. No general trace threshold is authorised.

The hazardous-waste helper retains the two operator shares at 0.5 kg each and all
four original freight entries. The inspected operators specify 25% wet-water
content and 17 MJ/kg lower heating value. Both allocate all burdens to disposal,
with no energy substitution. The complete native Swiss hazardous-incineration
service represents each share, separately. Its operating, residue, emission,
infrastructure and allocation model replaces source internals once; no source
plant emissions, cement, landfill or energy inventory is added on top. The source
without/with-energy-recovery inventories differ, and the native energy prose
swaps thermal/electric labels relative to source. These differences remain warned;
no energy credit or moisture/heating-value factor is invented.

The parent market and direct Swiss treatment already have factor -1, consistent
with negative source waste references and a positive native service reference.
Keep that factor once, including signed caller uncertainty and formulas.

Freight quantities match the earlier 3.9 helpers, but every inspected 3.10 freight
exchange has different uncertainty scales. The fresh helpers retain those actual
3.10 fields. Earlier 3.9 helpers, uncertainty, omission audits, shared reviews and
all other existing recipes are unchanged. Two raw profile lists gain explicit
3.10 target overrides, with qualified structural fingerprint migrations.

Older authoring utilities recognise only these exact, content-bound extensions;
an arbitrary added profile or changed fresh helper still fails validation. Their
component checks remain strict. The hazardous/paint authoring test uses its
proper historical stage: two RoW heat rules were superseded later in generator
order. Immutable later-review records reproduce the exact earlier binding hashes
in an isolated fixture; actual migration tests still use the final resource.

All native upstream exclusions and unquantified proxy effects remain limitations.
This work does not enable the public heat update.
