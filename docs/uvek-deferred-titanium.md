The reviewed scope is ecoinvent 3.9 cut-off titanium metal, not titanium dioxide.

reference and caller factor +1, with original signs, formula and uncertainty.

sponge market retains CN/RoW/JP shares of 0.425/0.33/0.245 and all freight.

utilities, factory and direct emissions. Recovered magnesium and chlorine use

+1 kg reference; stale comments about one kilogram of MgCl2 do not override

natural and synthetic Becher/Benelite rutile routes retain their market shares

Mining versus slag/smelting/synthetic routes, iron/vanadium co-product allocation,
