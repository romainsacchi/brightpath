Deferred waste-market reconstruction
====================================

fractions and freight through positive-reference service helpers. A negative
one-kilogram source waste reference becomes a positive one-kilogram service:
caller factor -1, with signed quantities, formulas and supported uncertainty
transformed once. No other release, reverse or consequential route is approved.

treatment has zero burdens.
