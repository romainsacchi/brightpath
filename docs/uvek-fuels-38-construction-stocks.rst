Construction-stock screening proxies
===================================

Three exact ecoinvent 3.8 cut-off references use complete native UVEK products
without changing migration rules or native inventories. Signed caller quantities,
uncertainty and formulas remain at factor +1 in their declared units.

* Hot-rolled 18/8 stainless steel uses the native complete mix of 63% converter
  and 37% electric steel plus one kg hot rolling per kg finished product.
  It is not an additional rolling service or unrolled stock.
* Reinforcing steel uses the native European 30% converter/70% electric mix.
  Both complete furnace routes include one kg rolling; the electric route uses
  its electric-steel rolling process. No second forming operation or diameter,
  length or strength conversion is added. A specific B500/B550 grade is not
  established by this proxy.
* Sole-plate/foundation concrete uses the complete native Swiss process per
  cubic metre as a deliberately different binder/formulation proxy. The source
  describes CEM I/II concrete with 355 kg cement and 2400 kg density per m³;
  native describes CEM III/B 32.5 slag cement, 325 kg cement and 2385 kg density.
  Strength, durability, exposure, pile performance and score equivalence are
  not established. Neither cement content nor density is a conversion factor.

Native production, input transport, allocation and infrastructure remain once.
Source market shares, final delivery and internal uncertainty are replaced,
not reconstructed. Geography, technology, vintage and native biosphere
exclusions remain limitations; score differences, especially concrete binder
changes, are unquantified. Separate caller transport and emissions remain.

The packaged ``uvek_fuels_38_construction_stocks_review.json`` binds scalar
reference facts, exact native rolling identities and witness hashes. Raw
licensed source inventories remain private. Component qualification does not
establish a successful full fuel update or export, or authorize other releases.
