# Wet acrylic recipes without guessed solution factors

The exact ecoinvent 3.9 cut-off dispersion and binder production exchanges each
reference one kilogram of wet product. Their declared dry contents are 58% and
54%. The dispersion market instead reports 59%, while old market comments still
mention 65% dispersion and 34% binder. Native legacy formulations have conflicting
concentration labels and “100% base” comments; incomplete precursor inventories
do not resolve those physical references. A multiplier inferred from the labels
would be unjustified.

The reconstruction therefore uses the actual source wet recipes. The generated
binder supplies the dispersion at the source wet-kg coefficient. Complete native
butyl acrylate, versatic-acid esters and vinyl acetate supply the binder, together
with the source utilities, factory, waste treatment and direct flows. The source
pigment, other chemicals, water withdrawal, utilities and waste supply dispersion
production. Caller signed wet kilograms, formulas and supported uncertainty use
factor +1. Neither complete legacy acrylic formulation is used directly; no
invented carrier-water addition, dewatering, drying or yield correction is added.

Two incorrect heuristic matches are corrected locally. Source 1-butanol uses
native 1-butanol, rather than the 2-butanol isomer. Each waste-emulsion-paint market
retains its hazardous/municipal/sanitary/inert treatment shares and original
freight, rather than using a single construction landfill. Complete native
0%-water services match the source dry-waste basis. The Swiss municipal route
lacks source fly-ash extraction and associated metal recovery. Hazardous branches
with/without source energy recovery use the complete native HWI boundary. These
technology, residue and allocation differences remain explicit; no replacement
recovery or avoided-energy credit is invented.

The main pigment uses native titanium-dioxide production mix, retaining both
chloride and sulfate routes rather than arbitrarily choosing only one. All
market-delivery layers remain, and rail remains rail. Complete native suppliers
retain their own infrastructure and internal transport. Chemical technologies,
factory capacity/lifetime, heat-market composition/delivery, waste composition,
fleets, market versus at-plant supply, allocation, geography, vintage and native
internal uncertainty remain screening approximations.

Nine direct source flows use matching publisher UUIDs, units, compartments and
roles in the canonical ecoinvent 3.10 biosphere. Dissolved and suspended solids
remain distinct; BOD/COD/DOC/TOC remain separate overlapping indicators, rather
than being added as independent material masses. The two original water
withdrawals remain in source m³. Two inherited, reviewed production-only
paint-to-clinker handovers remain literal audits; this makes no physical claim
that co-processing is burden-free. No new source exchange is omitted, and
original shares are not renormalized.

Six new helpers reuse twelve existing reviewed helpers, including the audited
allocation stock. A negative stock supply is allowed only for the previously
verified empty positive-reference recovered stock; treatment demand uses the
correct source reference sign. Native background content and its 2,991 excluded
biosphere exchanges remain unchanged. Source recipe completeness and effects on
scores remain uncertain; no modern industry or LCIA equivalence is claimed.

The hash-pinned review records actual source references, both rejected legacy
formulations, complete component witnesses, canonical direct-flow witnesses and
literal inherited audits. Tests cover wet quantities and source immutability,
monomer amounts, isomer identity, retained water/solids indicators, waste routes,
uncertainty, exact-release refusal, cold/warm behavior and atomic authoring.
Native LCI and public encrypted scenario/export/readback provide separate
integration evidence. Final goal packaging verification remains due.
