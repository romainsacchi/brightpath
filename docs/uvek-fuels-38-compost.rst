Allocated recovered compost and delivery
========================================

The inspected ecoinvent 3.8 cut-off compost market is not equivalent to UVEK's
complete composting process. Its six positive compost outputs contain 44
biosphere records whose amounts and locations are all exactly zero, with
uncertainty type zero and no dispersion, bounds or formulas. Each output shares
its publisher activity with a separately verified negative-reference waste
service carrying nonzero exchanges. This is an observed allocation boundary,
not a missing inventory or a small-value threshold.

The migration therefore creates an explicit recovered-compost handover with no
upstream inputs, while preserving all six source producer shares and both final
rail/road delivery entries separately. Freight quantities and uncertainty are
unchanged; native European transport fleets are documented geographic and
technology proxies. Original floating-point shares are not renormalized.

The positive reference remains one kg fresh compost. Negative caller quantities
remain product credits and are not flipped merely because the original supplier
name contains "treatment". The separate negative-reference waste services are
not changed. No composting burden, nutrient-equivalence factor, moisture or
dry-mass conversion, soil-carbon credit or recovery credit is invented. The
consumer's properties and separately listed application/emissions remain intact.

This does not claim actual composting is impact-free. Native UVEK's nonempty
``xx Compost, at plant`` contains collection, process energy, emissions and
infrastructure, and would change the inspected allocation if substituted here.
The recovered-stock helper is limited to these exact positive 3.8 co-products.
Every empty-stock dependency binds its exact direct supplier's reviewed profile;
other versions and waste references cannot borrow that approval.

Frozen witnesses and native-freight recipes are packaged in
``export/uvek_fuels_38_compost_review.json``. Raw licensed source vectors remain
private. The flattened complete-delivery LCI is finite and source databases are
unchanged; full fuel-sector preparation/export remains a separate qualification.
