Harvested rapeseed and sugar-beet sowing seed
============================================

The exact ecoinvent 3.8 cut-off rapeseed market retains all six producer entries
and all four final freight entries separately, with original quantities,
uncertainty and order. Nothing is omitted or renormalized. Native conventional
German, French Barrois and US farm inventories replace their corresponding
country entries. No native country match is established for Canada without
Quebec, CA-QC or RoW, so those three retain separate source shares but use the
complete US farm inventory as an explicit geographic screening proxy.

This is harvested oilseed in fresh kg, not propagation seed or an MJ-based
cultivation service. The source producers have different moisture contents;
CA-QC declares 9% water while the native US inventory documents 6%. Preserve the
original fresh-kg quantities without inventing moisture, yield, dry-matter,
oil-content or calorific-value multipliers. Canadian quality ratings, land-use
change, yields, irrigation, farm inputs, emissions, internal uncertainty and
regional production are not reproduced by the US proxy. Complete native farming
and drying remain inside each farm inventory; source final delivery is retained
separately using complete native ocean freight, rail, road and barge services.
Geographic, fleet, allocation, technology and vintage differences are warnings,
and score effects remain unquantified.

Sugar-beet sowing seed uses complete native Swiss IP propagation seed at regional
storage per kg fresh seed. Its existing preparation, dressing, packaging, storage
and transport are retained once. No root-crop, seed-count, additional dressing,
drying or delivery conversion is introduced. The global source and Swiss native
model have different sourcing and inventory assumptions, with explicit warnings;
this is not inventory or impact equality.

Frozen source witnesses and derived native stock/freight correspondences are in
``export/uvek_fuels_38_crops_review.json``. The sugar-beet migration rule remains
unchanged; its exact Premise profile requires separate reviewed authorization.
Source consumer quantities, formulas, uncertainty and separately listed
operations remain unchanged. Raw licensed source vectors remain private.
