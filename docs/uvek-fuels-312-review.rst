Fuel dependency mappings: cut-off 3.12
====================================

This review corrects seven inventory dependencies used in the shared Premise
fuel workflow. It changes mapping resources and their maintainer replay step,
without adding a Brightpath runtime adapter or an alternative Premise sector
engine. It does not certify fuel-sector transformation or export.

Delivered natural gas
---------------------

The actual high- and low-pressure source products explicitly state a net
calorific value of 36 MJ per standard cubic metre at 15 C and 1013 mbar.
Each new helper retains the original one-cubic-metre caller basis and supplies
36 MJ of the complete native RER gas-at-consumer service at the matching pressure.
The low-pressure case no longer selects gas at production. Native production,
transport and distribution remain complete; no extra burner, network, loss,
density, ideal-gas or carbon correction is invented. Source and native volume
states, composition, regional supply and vintage remain screening limitations.
The separate 3.6 high-pressure calibration of 38.256575270163715 MJ is preserved
only for its earlier inspected profile.

Water and wastewater
--------------------

The European tap-water group retains the actual two non-Swiss/Swiss shares,
their source order, quantities and uncertainty through complete native delivered
utilities. Source treatment portfolios and internal loops are replaced by those
native utility models, without another loss or water-balance adjustment.

The wastewater market retains its three routing coefficients and all 43 direct
emissions. The 3.9 and 3.12 outer vectors and parameters match after one label
update: Strontium becomes Strontium II, preserving publisher UUID
4295ed5b-9824-4bbf-97a4-fc4cabd87f0d, kg, surface-water compartment and quantity.
The existing helper already uses that exact canonical 3.10 definition. Its
volume-based native sewer and treatment component proxies remain complete;
source/native performance, composition, regional and lifetime differences remain
explicit limitations. No physical emission or treatment branch is omitted.

Materials and hydrogen
----------------------

AE7 retains its separate coconut-oil, palm-kernel-oil and petrochemical supplier
shares through the corresponding complete native AE7 factories. All three barge,
lorry and train freight quantities remain. The previous coconut-only match does
not represent this market's feedstock mix.

Zinc metal uses complete native primary SHG zinc at regional storage, including
refining, slag and wastewater treatment. Beneficiated concentrate is not metal.
Global/European supply, allocation, co-product, freight and vintage differences
remain screening limitations, without an extra metal-recovery credit.

Hydrogen retains an explicit kilogram-based market interface. Complete native
Swiss 25-bar steam-methane reforming supplies its historical baseline as a
screening proxy for a source market containing 88.65867411151911% SMR and the
remaining refinery/industrial co-product hydrogen. Pressure, production route,
allocation and geography differ. No compression, liquefaction, heat or avoided
production credit is inferred. Premise must subsequently connect this declared
market interface through its ordinary IAM hydrogen-market logic; preparing this
helper alone is not evidence of that connection.

Scope and evidence
------------------

Only the exact 3.12 cut-off profiles are added. Earlier reviewed 3.6 and 3.9
selected targets, factors, reference decisions and helper closures remain
unchanged. The frozen review contains the original parents and review cards,
primary inventory/metadata fingerprints, native references, component bindings,
derived target recipes and explicit limitations. Independent consumers must
prove an earlier projection unchanged before refreshing a closed fingerprint.
Tests cover signs and uncertainty, exact unit/product/pressure boundaries,
retained branches/emissions/freight, profile refusals, prior projection retention,
atomic refusal of changed evidence/components and idempotent authoring replay.
No uninspected release, consequential or inverse route is authorized.
