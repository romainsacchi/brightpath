Three additional 3.8 chemical-stock proxies
==========================================

Exact ecoinvent 3.8 cut-off profiles for sodium methoxide, liquid nitrogen and
anhydrous liquid ammonia use the existing complete native chemical inventories
per signed kg of declared product. Caller uncertainty and formulas are preserved.
No migration rule, native inventory or production module is changed.

Sodium methoxide is a deliberate same-product technology proxy. The source
market has 20% Brown-Schlesinger co-product and 80% direct methanol/alkali
production; the available native inventory represents Brown-Schlesinger only.
This separately reviewed choice resolves the pending case excluded by the
previous chemical-stock review. It does not reconstruct the source technology
mix. Feedstocks, co-products, allocation, energy, wastewater, emissions,
infrastructure and final delivery differ, with substantial unquantified score
effects. Retain the whole native process once; do not add source direct-route
energy/emissions, apply source producer shares to caller kg, or invent a
borohydride recovery credit or solution-concentration adjustment.

Liquid nitrogen remains liquid and retains native cryogenic separation,
liquefaction, cooling and allocation to liquid nitrogen/oxygen/argon. There is
no gaseous-volume conversion or additional liquefaction service. The European
native process is a geographic proxy for the source RoW supply.

Anhydrous liquid ammonia remains anhydrous liquid NH3. The complete European
regional-storage inventory retains its native 85% steam-reforming and 15%
heavy-fuel-oil partial-oxidation split and transport once. These are internal
native shares, not caller conversion factors. No aqueous dilution, nitrogen-
content or density multiplier is introduced.

All three replace source market production shares, final delivery and internal
uncertainty rather than reproduce them. The differences in technology,
allocation, geography, vintage and inventory boundary remain explicit warnings.
No equivalent impact score is claimed. Three native supplier LCIs are finite;
the source database is unchanged. Frozen evidence is packaged in
``export/uvek_fuels_38_additional_chemicals_review.json``.
