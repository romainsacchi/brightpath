Delivered film and zeolite markets
=================================

Two exact ecoinvent 3.8 cut-off market helpers retain their two original
producer entries and four final-delivery legs, in their original order.
Signed caller kg, formulas and uncertainty are preserved. Internal amounts and
marginal uncertainty remain unchanged, without omission or renormalization.

Both RER and RoW production entries use complete native European production;
RoW is an explicit geographic proxy. Native LDPE film includes resin supply,
feedstock transport and film extrusion. Zeolite powder is a nonempty aggregate
of resource and emission flows, even though it has no direct technosphere
inputs. Neither receives an extra resin, extrusion, precursor, drying or energy
process. Powder is not a slurry; film is not raw resin or battery packaging.

Four original final-freight legs use native generic road freight, a 3.5-tonne
gross-weight fleet for light-commercial freight, rail and ocean container
shipping. These retain tonne-km, not fleet composition, payload, size, empty
returns or geographic equivalence. The small-delivery fleet is a screening
proxy. Native input transport remains distinct from final market delivery.
Native internal uncertainty, technology, allocation, vintage and upstream
biosphere exclusions remain limitations, with unquantified score effects.

Only inspected 3.8 uses these helpers. The previously approved 3.9 zeolite
route retains its original direct native at-plant supplier and factor +1;
its original review remains unchanged. Other uninspected versions are refused,
rather than borrowing a newly modelled 3.8 market. The packaged review binds
the exact sources, native references, scalar entries, hashes and limitations.
Raw licensed source inventories remain private.

The two weighted native LCIs are finite. This component evidence does not
establish a successful full fuel-sector transformation or export. No runtime
Python module changes are needed.
