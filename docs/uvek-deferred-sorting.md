Deferred concrete sorting
========================

The exact Swiss ecoinvent 3.6 cut-off concrete-sorting supplier has a negative
one-kilogram waste reference. UVEK's matching Swiss sorting inventory supplies a
positive one-kilogram service. The inspected descriptions cover the same inert
concrete, 2200 kg/m3 density, demolition equipment, dry sorting and final-disposal
boundary. Factor -1 converts the caller's negative waste quantity into a positive
treatment purchase. Normal/lognormal uncertainty, bounds and formulas follow the
same reference-sign change once.

The complete native inventory retains its own transport, treatment and emissions.
No density correction, second disposal burden, recovery credit, helper or new
omission is introduced. Source/native routing, fleets, emission definitions,
vintage and allocation differences remain explicit, with unquantified score
effects. Other releases and waste markets do not inherit this exact approval.

This restores one additional pinned Premise CLIC exchange. It does not qualify
the misleading landfill candidates for inert waste or concrete-market mixes:
those must retain their actual clinker handovers, recycling and freight evidence.
