# Partial paperboard treatment inventories

Only the exact ecoinvent 3.9 and 3.12 cut-off RoW waste-paperboard markets are
covered. Each has a negative one-kilogram wet-waste reference. The generated
positive-reference service reverses caller signs, formulas and supported
uncertainty consistently. It retains the original 0.125163193694194 municipal
incineration fraction and every original freight entry: three rail and one road
entry in 3.9, one road entry in 3.12. No share is renormalized.

Eleven nonempty open-burning, open-dump and unsanitary-landfill branches per
profile have no defensible native UVEK counterpart. Their combined fraction is
0.8748368063058053 (3.9), or 0.8748368063058052 (3.12). They are final unsupported
branch omissions, with literal source-input audits and warnings. Managed
incineration or landfill cannot represent these uncontrolled routes. Neither a
zero-impact claim nor an avoided-treatment credit is introduced. The two caller
exchanges are partially represented and must never be counted as fully restored.

The retained incineration uses the complete native Swiss packaging-cardboard
service per wet kilogram. Its name says 19.6% water, while its documentation says
104370 ppm (10.437%). Source 3.9 production states 19.6%; source 3.12 production
states 10.4367%, and its overall market composition differs. There is no inferred
dry-mass, heating-value or moisture multiplier. Composition, energy recovery,
ash, effluent, collection, allocation, geography and vintage differences remain
explicit screening limitations with unquantified score effects. Road and rail
use complete native fleets at unchanged original tonne-kilometres.

The review pins the native catalogue, exact source profiles and inventory
witnesses. Helpers are named `PARTIAL SCREENING:`. Forward migration reports all
22 literal omissions; the Premise manifest and database metadata report actual
applied partial mappings and omitted-branch counts, including cached runs.
Unused approval entries do not label unrelated datasets as partial. Other source
releases and consequential profiles remain outside these exact approvals.

Focused tests cover positive, negative and zero caller quantities, normal and
lognormal uncertainty, literal omissions, source immutability, exact-profile
refusals and authoring drift. Native LCI and public scenario/export/readback are
separate integration checks. These mappings do not establish impact completeness
or scientific equivalence.
